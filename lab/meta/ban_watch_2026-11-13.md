# Ban watch: announced Friday 13 Nov 2026, effective 23 Nov

Noted 10 Nov 2026 from Jeremy's link to LotharHS's interview of Dave Guskin (Riftbound game director) at Regional
Championship Europe, Stuttgart: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lEeidrVN_Wo

**The video itself was not read.** YouTube answered this environment with its rate-limit page, and nothing was done
to get round it. What follows comes from written coverage of the same statements, not from the interview.

## THE ANNOUNCEMENT (official, read 13 Nov 2026 16:30 UTC)

"Radiance Ban List Updates (Effective November 23, 2026)", published 13 Nov 16:00 UTC, by Dave Guskin:
https://riftbound.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/announcements/radiance-ban-list-updates-effective-november-23-2026

Added to the Standard and Constructed 2v2 ban list, **effective Monday 23 November 2026**:

- Cards: **Riven, Broken Blade**; **Zhonya's Hourglass**; **Stellacorn Herder**; **Nocturne, Horrifying**;
  **Rhasa the Sunderer**; **Brynhir Thundersong**; **Dazzling Aurora**; **Baited Hook**.
- Battlefields: **Emperor's Dais**; **Star Spring**.

Riot's reasons, in short: Riven was 43% of the European Regional Championship, 3 of the top 4, about 60% against
the field outside the mirror (Broken Blade: a no-risk turn-two play; Hourglass: protection that hides from gear
removal, with Astral Heron; Herder: repeatable draw; the two battlefields: no-risk conquers). Kennen: 15% of the
field, 3 of the top 8 and the winner (Nocturne and Rhasa: free plays whose high rolls get worse with every
"look at the top"/"fill the trash" card). Kai'Sa: Brynhir locks the opponent's turn. Dazzling Aurora: "one of
the boogeymen", pushes every ramp deck into one form. Baited Hook: pre-emptive, for cards coming in Legacy.

### What it hits in the lab (checked against the files, 13 Nov)

- **Every Riven list** (23 of 23 tournament lists, all of Jeremy's: `decks/jeremy/*`, `decks/riven_best_field.txt`,
  `decks/riven_sacramento.txt`): the chosen champion itself (Riven, Broken Blade), 2-3 Stellacorn Herder, both
  battlefields, and 3 Zhonya's Hourglass where played. Riven needs a new champion and a new shell.
- **Kennen**: 7 of 7 tournament lists (3 Nocturne and 3 Rhasa in Evansrhim's). **Kai'Sa**: 4 of 4 (3 Brynhir in
  Jason Tian's). **Master Yi** (PCQ): 2 Hourglass and both battlefields. Rek'Sai (Rajan Nandan): nothing.
- **Volibear**: soldog's list plays 3 Dazzling Aurora (and so did the 12 Nov finalists). Ritualo's list: none in the
  main deck (3 in its sideboard). Laggy's list: none. `lab/variants/volibear-noaurora-best.txt` and the runner-up
  (Ritualo with 2 Rengar): none: both stay legal.
- **Evelynn** (Jeremy's list of 13 Nov): none.
- Jeremy's assumption of 13 Nov ("continue en assumant que Dazzling Aurora est ban") was right.

### What it means for the lab's work

- Until Sunday 22 Nov the current lists are legal and every result so far stands for that format.
- From 23 Nov the FIELD the searches rate lists against no longer exists: five of its six lists lose cards
  (both Rivens their champion). No tournament has been played without these cards, so there is no real
  post-ban list to import yet. A post-ban field has to be built (each legend's list with its banned cards replaced,
  then searched), or waited for.
- The engine's own legality flag (`CardDef::banned`, `src/cards/…`) is NOT changed yet: it would mark every Riven
  list of the lab illegal at once. To do on or before 23 Nov, on Jeremy's word.

## What is confirmed (by Guskin, via written sources)

- A ban announcement is due **Friday 13 November 2026**; the date the bans take effect is still under discussion.
- **No ban takes effect on the weekend of 14-15 November** (events that weekend, including the $50K CCS, use the
