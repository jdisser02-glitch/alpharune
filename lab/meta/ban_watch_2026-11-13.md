# Ban watch: announcement due Friday 13 Nov 2026

Noted 10 Nov 2026 from Jeremy's link to LotharHS's interview of Dave Guskin (Riftbound game director) at Regional
Championship Europe, Stuttgart: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lEeidrVN_Wo

**The video itself was not read.** YouTube answered this environment with its rate-limit page, and nothing was done
to get round it. What follows comes from written coverage of the same statements, not from the interview.

## What is confirmed (by Guskin, via written sources)

- A ban announcement is due **Friday 13 November 2026**; the date the bans take effect is still under discussion.
- **No ban takes effect on the weekend of 14-15 November** (events that weekend, including the $50K CCS, use the
  current format).
- No card has been named by Riot.
- Reported topics of the interview (LotharHS's own summary, not read in detail): the metagame, leaks about Legacy,
  balance learnings, reprints.

## Update, 13 Nov 2026 00:30 UTC

- **Any ban takes effect on Monday 23 November 2026**; until Sunday 22 the current cards are legal and no
  scheduled event is touched. Source: riftbound.zone, 10 Nov
  (https://riftbound.zone/ban-list-ban-in-vigore-23-novembre/), reporting a post by Dave Guskin on X (the post
  itself was not read). Guskin speaks of "any bans": there may be none.
- Checked at 00:30 UTC: nothing published yet. The official news page
  (https://riftbound.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/) still has "September Ban List Updates" as its latest ban
  article; riftbound.zone, piltoverarchive.com and riftatlas.com carry no announcement.

## Context

- Stuttgart day 1: 126 of 291 decks were Riven (43.3%); day 2: 47 of 96; Top 8: four Riven, three Kennen, one
  Kai'Sa; winner Evansrhim on Kennen.
- Previous bans: 31 March 2026 (Called Shot, Draven Vanquisher, Fight or Flight, Scrapheap, three battlefields);
  15 September 2026 (Ekko Recurrent, Stacked Deck). Guskin's stated line: bans as a last resort.
